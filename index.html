Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about reservations, date changes, and curated tours.

All reservations made through WanderStay include complimentary cancellation or date rescheduling up to 48 hours prior to your scheduled check-in date. You can manage or cancel your active bookings anytime from the My Bookings drawer in the top navigation bar.

Children under 6 years stay complimentary when sharing existing bedding with parents. For groups exceeding 2 adults per room, a nominal extra-guest hospitality supplement is automatically calculated in your live fare summary.

In Step 4 of the booking wizard, you can apply WANDER20 for 20% off your subtotal, FIRST50 for a flat ₹5,000 instant discount on stays above ₹20,000, or FESTIVE15 for a 15% seasonal holiday discount.

WanderStay uses browser localStorage persistence via ES6 state management. Your confirmed e-tickets, selected currency, and theme preferences remain intact across page refreshes, and you can download a printable itinerary voucher for every reservation.
